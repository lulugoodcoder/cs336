(e) Take GPT-2 XL and increase the context length to 16,384. How does the total FLOPs for one
forward pass change? How do the relative contribution of FLOPs of the model components
change?
Deliverable: A one-to-two sentence response.

When increasing context length from 1,024 to 16,384, total FLOPs increase by ~33× (from 4.5T to 149T). 
The attention score computation (QK^T and Attention×V) grows from 7% to 55% of total FLOPs due to its O(T²) complexity, 
while SwiGLU drops from 67% to 32%, explaining why long-context models require optimizations like Flash Attention.
